NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between the Disclosing Party and the Receiving Party.

1. Definition of Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means any and all non-public, proprietary, or confidential information disclosed by the Disclosing Party.

2. Obligations of Receiving Party. The Receiving Party agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not disclose it to any third party.

3. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware.

4. Term. The obligations under this Agreement shall survive for a period of five years from the date of disclosure.
