MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between the Client and the Provider.

1. Services. The Provider shall perform the services described in the applicable Statement of Work.

2. Payment. The Client shall pay all fees within thirty days of receipt of invoice.

3. Arbitration. Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved by binding arbitration in New York under the rules of the American Arbitration Association.

4. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon ninety days written notice.
